Official Privacy Policy & Data Protection Guidelines

Google Play Developer Policy Compliant

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Welcome to VR Tuition ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy is formulated to inform users about the collection, usage, and safeguarding of information when using the VR Tuition mobile application (the "App") and our official platform (vracademy.reachawave.in).

1. Information We Collect

To ensure a seamless educational experience, attendance tracking, and academic updates, we collect minimal and necessary information:

A. Personal Information

  • Account Credentials: Student/Parent name, registered mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Academic Profile: Class, enrolled subjects, batch timings, and academic performance records.

B. Device & Diagnostic Data

  • Log Data: Device model, operating system version, app version, network connectivity, and crash logs to resolve bugs and improve application reliability.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process personal and academic records strictly for legitimate educational operations:

  • Enabling student login and identity authentication.
  • Sharing study materials, schedules, test announcements, and marks notifications.
  • Offering direct communication and customer support for parents and students.
  • Ensuring security, preventing unauthorized access, and maintaining infrastructure stability.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade student or personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Children’s Privacy

As an educational portal, VR Tuition respects the privacy of students and minors. Access for students is managed under parental or institutional supervision. We do not solicit personal information beyond what is strictly necessary for academic tracking.

4. Data Storage and Security

All sensitive information is transmitted using secure standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and housed in guarded cloud environments. We implement industry-grade technical and organizational measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, modification, or exposure.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We keep user information only as long as an active enrollment remains in place or as required for accounting and verification. Users and parents can request complete deletion of their account records at any time by writing to our support team.

6. Third-Party Integrations

The application connects securely to our central web servers (vracademy.reachawave.in). We may employ trusted core services (such as push notification providers) that adhere strictly to stringent security and privacy regulations.
